The problem

Industrial renewable portfolios must be re-planned every 15 minutes under deep uncertainty

Volatile, messy inputs

  • Solar & wind swing with clouds and storms
  • IEX prices change every 15-min block
  • Crucial facts arrive as images, PDFs and emails: inspection photos, work orders, grid notices

Conflicting goals

  • Lowest cost vs cleanest energy vs never cutting critical load
  • The right trade-off depends on the company's risk appetite

Today's practice

  • Rule-of-thumb control: charge on surplus, discharge on deficit
  • Reacts only to now; ignores forecasts, prices, documents and risk
  • Breaks grid-line limits, wastes clean power

Business-as-usual on our test portfolio (5 solar, 3 wind, 2 batteries, 4 industrial loads)

  • ₹94.2 lakh per day energy cost · 957 t CO₂ per day · only 62% clean energy
  • 12.1 MWh of critical load cut per day on average (on 20 of 100 days) · 4.9 physical-limit violations per day

Adjusting for uncertainty: protection scales with probability

Same day, same time — only the storm probability changes

F2 · F3

Why it matters

A fixed playbook treats every storm alert alike. Here the storm is sampled into ~p% of 100 futures, so protection rises gradually with risk — the AI pays for protection only when the expected outage cost justifies it.

What the agent says (real output)

I tested 33 combinations from 9 recipe bands across 100 possible futures and chose a 'Protective' plan (keep ≥52% battery reserve) … 'Max savings' would save money but risks a critical shortfall in 50% of futures (limit 2%).

Combined risks are handled together: a storm, a price spike, a battery fault and a grid notice all enter the same 100 futures — nothing is collapsed into one 'situation'.

Agentic & autonomous capability

Reasons, plans, uses tools, acts, recovers from errors and learns

Agentic

Reasons

Diagnoses 9 interacting risk signals plus image & document evidence; writes its own rationale.

Plans

24 h day-ahead plan + rolling 4 h plans, re-planned every 15 min and instantly after shocks or new documents.

Uses tools

MILP optimiser, Monte Carlo simulator, physics validator, image analysers, document parser, weather / satellite / price APIs, crew dispatch.

Acts autonomously

Dispatches assets, reduces flexible load, reschedules maintenance, sends repair crews after breakdowns or thermal hot-spots.

Recovers from errors

Infeasible → relax ×2 → safe fallback · API down → last-year proxy → replay → simulated · LLM failure → optimiser decides · bad files → rejected.

Learns & self-critiques

Learns forecast bias daily; the LLM can challenge the ladder by proposing its own plan, which must win on the same stress test.

Responsible AI, human oversight & robustness

Autonomous by design — governed by limits, verification, override and audit

Responsible AI

No hallucinated actions

The LLM can only propose settings or pick a validated plan id; all MW come from the optimiser; ineligible picks are rejected in code.

Multimodal guardrails

Every image / document finding is validated and confidence-scored; below 75% it is quarantined for a human, never applied.

Human oversight

Operator override hands control to the safe controller instantly; high-impact actions are flagged for review; management sets risk limits and recipe ranges.

Physics guardrails

Validator checks state of charge, rates, line limits, balance, critical load and availability before every action; the twin independently counts violations.

Transparency & audit

Every decision logged: signals, evidence, 33 options, stress results, ladder, action, explanation — reproducible with seeded scenarios.

Security & privacy

No personal data; keys only in .env; read-only SCADA first; OT isolation; shadow-mode rollout before control.

Scalability & path to a real plant

Industry-agnostic by configuration; one interface swaps the twin for live data

Scale

Week 0 · Configure

Assets, loads, schedules, targets (UI, YAML or CSV); upload documents & images.

Weeks 1–2 · Shadow mode

SCADA / meters / BMS read-only (OPC-UA, Modbus, MQTT); AI recommends, operators compare.

Weeks 3–4 · Battery control

Battery set-points only; watchdog returns to local control on silence.

Month 2+ · Full autonomy

Curtailment & demand response; learning tunes forecasts per site.

  • Any number of farms, batteries and loads — sites without wind or batteries are tested
  • < 1 s per decision for 33 plans × 100 futures on a laptop; solves run in parallel
  • Same agent for steel, cement, textiles or data centres — only configuration changes
  • New modalities plug into the same extract → validate → gate → apply pipeline

Honest limitations & roadmap

What we have not proven yet, and what comes next

  • Thermal images are synthetic with known ground truth (no real drone data available); satellite scenes are real
  • No audio / voice input yet
  • Plant meters, batteries and demand come from a physics-based digital twin; weather and satellite are live
  • IEX price file is a labelled sample
  • Results produced in offline mode; the LLM's added value is not yet measured on the 100 days
  • Band search finds very good plans, not a proven global optimum
  • Real drone / thermal datasets and panel-defect models
  • Voice notes from field crews → the same document pipeline
  • Live SCADA adapter with data-health monitoring
  • Real IEX DAM + RTM prices and DSM penalty rules
  • A/B the LLM layer over 100 days with a free-tier model
  • Full stochastic optimisation as the end-state optimum
